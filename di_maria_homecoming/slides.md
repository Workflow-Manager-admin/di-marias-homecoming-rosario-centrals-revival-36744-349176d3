# Return Announcement

![Rosario Central Fans](https://imagenes.lacapital.com.ar/files/image/343/343888/60d9f30850c15.jpg)
<div style="font-size:1.6rem; color: #0033A0; font-weight: bold; margin-top: 1rem;">Official Return Confirmed!</div>

- **Date:** June 2024
- **Statement:**  
  > "I always dreamed of returning to Rosario Central, my home. Now is the moment to give the fans and the club everything I have."  
  — Ángel Di María

- **Unveiling:**  
  Grand event at Estadio Gigante de Arroyito, massive community turnout, media coverage throughout Argentina.
